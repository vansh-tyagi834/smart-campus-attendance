

### **PHASE 2: Advanced Analytics Features**

#### **2.1 Department Comparison View**
- Side-by-side comparison cards
- Best/Worst performing departments
- Monthly trends per department
- Faculty-wise breakdown

#### **2.2 Predictive Analytics** 🔮
- "At-risk prediction" - Students likely to drop below 75%
- ML-based attendance forecasting
- Alert system for HOD

#### **2.3 Time-Range Filter**
- Dropdown: Last 7 days, Last month, Last semester, Custom range
- Update all charts dynamically
- Export filtered data

### **PHASE 3: Management Tools**

#### **3.1 Faculty Management**
- Add/Remove faculty members
- Assign subjects to faculty
- View faculty attendance records
- Performance metrics

#### **3.2 Bulk Actions**
- Select multiple students
- Bulk email/SMS alerts
- Mass mark attendance (emergency)
- Export selected records

#### **3.3 Notification Center** 🔔
- Bell icon with badge count
- Real-time alerts (low attendance, device offline)
- Mark as read/unread
- Filter by type

#### **4.1 Auto-Generated Reports** 📄
- Weekly summary email to HOD
- Monthly attendance reports
- Semester-end analytics PDF
- Export with college logo/seal

#### **4.2 Custom Report Builder**
- Select parameters: Date range, Department, Attendance %
- Choose charts to include
- Download PDF/Excel

#### **4.3 Comparison Reports**
- Compare current vs previous semester
- Year-over-year trends
